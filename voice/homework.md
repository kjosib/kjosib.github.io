# About Homework:

Homework is "assigned" in the sense that if you do it, I think it will help you.
I won't force you to do it, but regular daily *practice* makes regular daily *progress.*

When I ask for recordings, that generally means I plan to spend a few minutes to review those recordings and offer feedback.
It may not happen the same day, but it's the plan. That can enable early course corrections and help prevent missteps.

Please reach out if you have technical difficulties doing the homework.

Lastly, please do not treat the homework as an obstacle. Life happens.
If the homework doesn't get done, so be it. You're still invited to come to the next lesson.

(assuming your bills are paid, that is.)

# General Structure of Homework:
- Text: explaining what your *intention* is for the practice session
- Sound: The most relevant 30-60 seconds of *the actual recording*
- Text: Your own *evaluation* of the result
- Text: Your *plans* for the following day
