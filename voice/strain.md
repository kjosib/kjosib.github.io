# Strain

Relaxation facilitates gliding between pitches.
Strain tends to make it rough, rigid, choked, disjointed, hard to change (e.g. loudness).

Naturalness: At least two of harmonicity / lack of strain-implying features, consistency, and emotiveness.

Gold standard test of strain: Balanced onsets throughout pitch range.

Tackle strain where it's just marginally coming into the voice; *not* in the region of its clearly-obvious presentation.
By growing the green zone, the orange and red will both move.

G-sound helps with closure.
B-sound is less effective.

## Treatment:

1. Aim for the right sound. You need to have a clear concept of a clean tone.
2. Meta-strategy: Lean into the wrong and correcting it.
3. Deliberately tense and untense muscles - even whole body; hold; relax; add pitch.
