# Distortion

Types:
* Rattling: Growl and Rasp
* Fry
* Fry screaming (which is evidently different)?
* Ingressive sounds

Rattles:
* Rasp - comes from FVF
* Growl - from epiglottal trill (and maybe arytenoid cartileges trilling, Jana said?)

(Epiglottal) Rattle: growly sound

Knoedel: tongue goes back and down.
