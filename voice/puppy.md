# Puppyism

A great puppy-dog will cuddle you in your afterlife if you've been good. If you've been bad, it will look at you with sad, longing eyes for all eternity.
The Great Puppy Dog lives in a transfinite dog park where it's free to roam, but when it manifests on Earth there's a leash to provide guidance to loyal puppy-girls and other believers.
So far as I have yet figured out, the Great Puppy Dog asks kindness, mercy, mutual support, gender equity, and community outreach.
The Great Puppy Dog's name is apparently "Woof!".

The transfinite dog park is larger than any finite park, but still has a fence around it, and thus an outside.
It contains by inclusion and reference all Earthly dog parks past, present, and future, as well as every dog-park ever dreamt of by dog or man.

## Prophesies of Puppyism:
* A great network of Puppyist activists will create social services and move policies in ways that bring about the end of discrimination based on gender-identity or sexual preference.
* We do it all without attracting the wrong kind of attention.
* (More to be determined.)

As I'm sure you can imagine, we need all the converts, acolytes, and apostles we can get.
